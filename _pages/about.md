I am a Computer Engineering graduate from the University of Sri Jayewardenepura, Sri Lanka, with a major in Computer Engineering and a minor in Data Management. My interests include machine learning, computer vision, edge AI, robotics, and scientific machine learning applied to science.

I currently work as a Temporary Demonstrator in the Department of Electrical & Computer Engineering at The Open University of Sri Lanka. I also volunteer as a Research Assistant at SLIIT, where I study thin-film degradation using Optical Coherence Tomography images.

Previously, I completed a six-month placement as a Trainee Full-Stack Developer and DevOps Engineer at Sri Lanka Telecom PLC. My work included MERN-stack development, REST APIs, MongoDB, PDF generation, CI/CD, and Kubernetes deployments.

My recent projects include an edge-AI elderly-care robot using ROS 2 and NVIDIA Jetson, a Lorentz-equivariant transformer for high-energy collision classification, a microservices-based smart gas distribution platform, and a Sri Lankan car-price prediction system.
